General Rules


   Please read all rules before attending an Airbase Volleyball pickup game. We will be focusing on equality, fairness, respect and having a good time playing
   volleyball.

  • Please keep the socializing and communication very chill, free of racism, politics, hate, sexism, etc
  • Take your garbage and drink bottles home with you
  • Please do not use outdoor shoes on the court
  • Keep the playing surface DRY, if it gets wet, try to clean or tell the organizer
  • Be a team player and try to share the court; it is dangerous to play a ball that is someone else's ball
  • Please do not use the pickup game to work on a difficult serve that 9 times out of 10 does not put the ball in play, you may work on difficult serves before actual games only
  • If you are an advanced player, please do not use the pickup game as serving practise; if the opposing team cannot handle 2-3 hard top spins / floaters, please reduce power on the next serve
  • Try your best not to land on someone's foot/ankle/leg/body after spiking or blocking; we don't need a rolled/broken ankle at a pickup game
  • Only advanced games and above will feature a dedicated setter, Beginner and Intermediate games will have the 'middle sets' rule at position 3 to ensure everyone can practise setting
  • If you cannot easily reach the 2nd ball, please call for help to avoid colliding/injuring another player, not every ball has to be touched
  • Friends that play together are welcome at Airbase Volleyball, but do not set to ONLY your friends, this will not be tolerated; everyone should be set equally
  • Please make sure to set the ball to front row players first before attempting to set back row players, unless front row players are unable to successfully attack or have asked to not have the ball set to them
  • Be honest with your skill level as a setter. The setter will touch the ball the most, so please do not abuse this amazing privilege; if you cannot consistently set teammates for a chance to attack on most passes, you will be asked to let someone else set
  • Setter dumps should only be used if there is no other way to play the ball, or if many points have been scored on the same rotation
